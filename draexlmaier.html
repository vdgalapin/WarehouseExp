Overview

At Draexlmaier, I work as a System Coordinator supporting warehouse and production operations through system visibility, reporting, process investigation, and cross-functional coordination. My role connects logistics, production, IT, linefeed, shipping, receiving, planning, and engineering so teams can act on accurate operational data.

I use plant systems and reporting tools to help make sure production has the correct materials, the correct part numbers, the right revision levels, and the visibility needed to keep operations running in sequence. I also investigate system and process issues that can affect inventory movement, quality, linefeed timing, and finished good accuracy.

Because the operation runs in a Just-in-Time and Just-in-Sequence environment, my work is focused on accuracy, timeliness, and keeping connected processes aligned across the plant.

Cross-Functional Coordination

I support operations across logistics, production, IT, shipping, and receiving by helping teams work from the same reliable system information. I communicate with planners, project engineering leads, engineering, linefeed, and shipping to make sure part numbers, production activity, and inventory data stay aligned.

This includes helping ensure production has something to run, checking that the right materials are flowing at the right time, and making sure reporting reflects what is actually happening inside the plant.

Reporting and Visibility

I export operational data from MES and related systems to show what planners created, what project teams are working on, and what finished goods are available or in process. I use Excel heavily to turn that data into faster, more useful reports for plant teams.

I also use macros to automate recurring reports so updates can be delivered more efficiently and consistently. Teams rely on me for accurate information because I have access to the systems they use and can connect the data across those tools.

Detailed Responsibilities and Coordination Work

1. Plant Reporting and Operational Communication

I use MES-MTOi and related tools to export production, planning, and finished goods information, then turn that data into reports the plant can act on. These reports help teams understand current status, production readiness, and where attention is needed across operations.

Because many groups depend on this information, accuracy matters. I help keep logistics, production, and support teams aligned by delivering system-backed visibility rather than relying on assumptions or incomplete updates.

2. Excel-Based Investigation and Report Automation

Excel is a major part of my workflow for both reporting and problem investigation. I use it to analyze issues, identify exceptions, and check for things that should not be happening in the plant systems or process flow.

I also use macros to automate recurring reports so plant updates can be created faster and with less manual effort. This improves consistency and helps teams respond more quickly to operational changes.

3. QC, Booking, and Inventory Accuracy Checks

I investigate inventory and quality-related exceptions, including QC cases where items are scrapped under the wrong codes. I also check for double booking in production, where parts may be booked incorrectly in order to influence reported numbers.

In addition, I validate inventory movement and corrections based on booking codes so that material flow and system records stay accurate. This helps reduce risk in reporting, inventory control, and production decision-making.

4. Linefeed Monitoring and Part Flow Control

I work with linefeed to make sure the correct part numbers are moving to the line and that requested parts are being supplied in a timely manner. Using StockObserver and PSM, I review movement activity, operational visibility, and performance tied to linefeed execution.

This matters because delays, wrong part usage, or poor movement control can directly affect production continuity in a JIT / JIS environment.

5. JIT / JIS Sequencing Support

Because the plant operates in a Just-in-Time and Just-in-Sequence model, timing and sequence are critical. I help monitor whether operations are staying aligned with current production needs so the plant can continue running without unnecessary disruption.

My work supports the discipline needed for sequence-sensitive operations where material flow, production timing, and system accuracy have to stay synchronized.

6. Revision Change Management

I also support revision changes from the production and shipping side, helping make sure the right finished goods are being produced and delivered based on current requirements. This includes internal running changes that may require label updates on our side, as well as harder customer-driven changes tied to Tesla demand.

I work with shipping to make sure parts are segregated correctly by revision level so the wrong material is not mixed, shipped, or used in production.

7. System Link Between PSM and MES

I work with HOST as the middle layer between PSM and MES, helping make sure both systems stay updated and aligned. This is important because planners, sales, and production all rely on consistent information across those systems to make decisions and execute correctly.
